Across this course, almost every measured number comes from two files. This unit is about those two files — what they say about themselves, and what the course's claims are therefore worth.

Both of them live inside the matplotlib package — demo data shipped with a plotting library. Not chosen for their scientific properties. Chosen because they are already on the machine of anyone who can run the code.

Why that was the right choice

It looks like a shortcut and it is defensible, for one reason that outweighs the rest: every figure in this course can be re-derived by anyone, on any machine, with no download, no credentials and no dataset that might move or disappear.

That is not a small property. A curriculum whose numbers rest on a file only its author can reach is a curriculum nobody can check — and this whole programme is built on the claim that its numbers are checkable.

The distinction in that last row is the one this unit exists to make explicit. The course teaches reads — how to ask a record whether it carries structure. Conclusions about brains or markets belong to whoever owns the subject matter, and the next two slides show where the line falls.

The first record does not say what shape it is

eeg.dat has no header, no units, no sampling rate and no channel names. It is 3,200 numbers in a row. Every unit in this course reads it as 800 rows of 4 — and 3,200 divides five ways the instrument will read without complaint:

At (1,600, 2) the instrument reports nothing there — no modes above the floor, attenuation exactly zero. That zero is the result. At (200, 16) it resolves 4 modes. Same bytes. Same instrument. Nothing in the file says which is right.

The highlighted row is the one every other unit uses. It is a reasonable choice — four channels is what an EEG file of this vintage usually holds — and it remains a choice, made by a reader, not a fact read out of the data.

Unit A8 goes one level further, folding that same reading into 100 trials of 8 steps across 4 electrodes, and says on its own slide that this is “a fold nothing in the file says is true”. This slide is that sentence one level lower down: the two-dimensional shape is an assumption too.

And the second says more than it is asked

Stocks.csv is better behaved: it has column names, dates, and a single comment line naming where it came from — Data source: https://finance.yahoo.com. That one line is more provenance than the first record carries in total.

in the fileas the course uses it
rows52471

span1990-01-01 → 2022-06-282016-09-01 → 2022-06-28

The course's 71 × 8 is what survives dropping every row where any one of the eight companies is missing. That is a defensible rule and it is also a filter: it selects the window in which all eight happen to be listed, which is the most recent 2016 of a file that starts in 1990.

Two of the file's 10 columns are not companies at all — ^GSPC, ^IXIC are market indices. They are not among the eight used, and a reader who took “all the columns” would be mixing two different kinds of thing on one screen without noticing.

None of that is a defect in the file. It is what happens when a record is used for something other than what it was published for, which is the normal case.

The second record's rule is a choice too

The shape table above asks the first record a hard question. The second deserves the same one. The course keeps every row where all 8 companies have a price — defensible, and a filter. Leave each company out in turn and watch what the filter keeps:

Seven of the eight change nothing at all — the window stays at 71 rows whichever of them is dropped. Removing DELL alone takes it to 215, 3.0 times the record, out of a file with 524 rows in it. The course's window is a property of one company, and the number 71 carries no record of that.

So the same file, read four defensible ways:

The structural answer does not move. Every reading resolves 1 mode. Whatever else the choice of columns does, it does not change the count of things the instrument finds in this record.

The quantities do move, and by a lot. Attenuation runs from 0.685292 to 1.372023 across those four rows — a factor of 2.0, on one file, with no row of it altered. A figure carried out of here without the reading that produced it is a number without a population.

Which is the first record's lesson arriving by a different road. There it was the shape that nothing in the file settled; here the shape is given and it is the selection that is not. Both are choices made by the reader, both are defensible, and neither is visible in the number that comes out the other end unless someone writes it down.

What the course's numbers are worth

Every figure in every one of these units is reproducible: re-derived from the running code each time a page is built, checked against the rendered page by a script that ships beside it, on data that anyone reading this already has.

reproducible  ≠  a claim about the world

The reads say what they say about these arrays of numbers. That the first record resolves 1 mode at (800, 4) is a fact about a file. It is not a finding about brain activity, and this course has never asked it to be — which is why every unit teaches a read and none teaches a conclusion.
