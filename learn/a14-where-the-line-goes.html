Entroptics / lesson A14 · the evidence under a count

Where the Line Goes

Every unit in this course reads Ksignal and reports it as a number of modes. This one opens it: a count needs a line, and the evidence the line is drawn through is published per mode.

For every singular value the instrument gives a deviate — how far it stands above what noise alone produces — and a tail probability, the chance noise alone would reach it. It publishes both together as significance, one row per mode. Here they are for the EEG read as 8 columns:

modedeviatep
mode 1+55.661.32e-94inside every line below
mode 2+29.511.31e-43inside every line below
mode 3+15.581.21e-19inside every line below
mode 4+2.951.83e-03inside 0.05, outside Ksignal's
mode 5-25.071.00e+00outside every line
mode 6-27.681.00e+00outside every line

The first three are past any line anyone would draw: a tail probability of 1e-19 means noise would produce that mode about once in every 8,287,338,414,761,033,728 tries. The fourth is the one to look at. Its p is 1.83e-03 — inside the library's documented false-alarm level of 0.05, and outside the count Ksignal reports.

Ksignal = 3  ·  modes with p < 0.05 = 4

Both are correct, and they answer two questions. A count needs a line; Ksignal draws one and the evidence is published so a reader can draw their own. On every record in this unit Ksignal is at least as strict as the documented level — it never reports more modes than p < 0.05 does.

Move the line, on eight records

On a record with nothing in it, every line agrees on zero. Both noise draws return 0 at all four levels and at Ksignal's. The choice cannot make a mode out of noise at any level on this table, which is the property that lets a reader trust the answer nothing here without knowing where the line was.

On a record with something in it, the count moves. The terrain grid reads 13, 13 or 15 modes; the EEG reads 1 or 2; the EEG at eight columns reads 3 or 4. The most any record moves across every line on this table is 2.

So the verdict survives the choice and the count does not. Unit A11 reaches that distinction by varying the amount of record; unit A12 reaches it by varying a gain. This is a third route to it, and what varies here is the reader: the record is the same in every column.

What to do with it

  • A count is a decision; the evidence is not. The deviates and tail probabilities are properties of the record. How many of them you call modes is a line, and somebody draws it.
  • Ask before quoting a count. On 5 of the 8 records here the count is the same wherever the line goes. On the other 3 it moves, and the mode in question is the one just past the edge — p = 1.83e-03 on the EEG read as 8 columns.
  • Nothing here manufactures a mode. Both noise records read zero at every level including Ksignal's, so a reader who is asking is there anything gets an answer that does not depend on the choice.
  • Ksignal is at least as strict as the documented level. On all 8 records here it reports no more modes than p < 0.05 does, so a count taken from it is the conservative one.

deviate and p per mode  →  a line  →  Ksignal

Unit A7 asked what nothing looks like and found the answer depends on the null. This is the same question one step later: given the null, and given the evidence it produces, the count still depends on where a reader draws a line. Both are choices, both are recorded, and neither is hidden — the evidence for every mode is one call away.
