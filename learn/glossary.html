The words

27 terms the course uses as if you already know them. The owner is the unit that introduces the word and is the place to read it properly; the last column is how many of the 50 units use it in prose, so you can see which words are load-bearing and which are local.

The one-line meanings are written by hand. Everything else on this page is measured when it is built, and the build fails if a unit named as an owner never actually says its word — a glossary pointing at the wrong lesson is worse than no glossary.

etendueHow much of the available space a signal actually occupies — a beam's spread, borrowed from optics and meaning the same thing here.A22
exact zeroThe result when a read finds nothing above the floor. It is a measured value, and the reads here return it in place of a small number so that a reader cannot mistake it for a faint effect.A1011
fillThe share of the screen a signal actually uses, as opposed to the share it was given.first seen in A1A29
floorThe level a pattern has to stand above to be counted. Derived from the record's own shape, never chosen.first seen in A1A735
instrumentThe library, and the reads it publishes. It has no model in it and nothing to fit, so what it returns is a property of the record and of the reading.A138
lensA name and two conversions — one that puts a surface onto the screen, one that takes it back off. Everything else about it is certified, not declared.first seen in A1B18
mass gapThe distance between a vacuum and the next energy level. Positive means correlations die at a definite rate, and a rate is what a mass is.D54
modeOne independent pattern in a record. The count of them above the floor is the single number most of this course turns on.A229
noise floorWhere a read stops finding anything. Modes above it are counted and modes below it are not, and where exactly it falls is a choice somebody makes.first seen in A1A49
nullA way of destroying exactly the structure you claim to be measuring, so that what a read returns on it is what 'nothing' looks like.first seen in A3A715
plaquetteOne square face of one cube on a lattice — the unit area is counted in, the way a floor is counted in tiles.first seen in D5D5a5
point-spreadHow far influence reaches along an ordered axis before it fades — the same arithmetic as survival over a distance, read along a different axis.first seen in A2A36
projectionReading a record against a direction, and asking how much of it lies along that direction.A17
recordThe thing being read: numbers in rows and columns, where the rows have an order and the columns are channels. What it is a record OF sits outside every read.A146
scatterThe part of a read that is its own channels disagreeing. It costs nothing to measure, because the channels are repeats of one measurement.A136
screenWhere a record is put so it can be read: rows in an order, columns as channels, everything on one shared basis.A125
spotsHow many things a reading can tell apart: one over the diffraction limit. A count of separable places, not of anything found in them.first seen in A2B56
strehlHow concentrated a signal is into its strongest single mode. Near 1 is one dominant pattern; near 0 is energy spread thin.A22
transferWhat actually crosses from one side of a screen to the other, bounded by the two beams' phase space and by nothing else.first seen in A10B25
verdictThe part of an answer that survives being asked again: whether a read clears the line, as against the magnitude it clears it by. The magnitude belongs to the read and the record together.first seen in A1A415
vortexA closed sheet made of plaquettes, carrying a twist in the field. Closed means it has no edge, like the surface of a balloon.first seen in D5D5a4
