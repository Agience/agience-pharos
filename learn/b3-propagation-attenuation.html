Entroptics / lesson seven · what travel costs

Propagation
and Attenuation

Six units taught the instrument on its own terms. This is where it stops being about matrices and becomes the reason the whole system is built the way it is.

The design canon does not treat these as metaphors. It says: “Decay is attenuation; forgetting is cooling. These are not policy that merely takes a measured input — they are the physics the instrument measures the constants of.”

Four different things wear the word “attenuation” here. They share one ceiling and they are not interchangeable, and the difference between two of them is the kind of mistake the code will not catch for you.

Mechanism one — a rate over a distance

Send something across a gap and some fraction of it arrives. The fraction is

survival  =  e−d / ξ

d is the distance travelled. ξ is the Greek letter xi, said KSY or ZY — here it is the correlation length, the distance over which the record stays like itself. e is the constant 2.718…, and a negative power of it is what shrinking smoothly toward zero looks like.

ξ is not chosen. It is read off the record. Unit A6 put the EEG's two halves on one screen; that screen's own read gives ξ = 4.467646, and that is the number used everywhere below. The library's own docstring says so: “read it from correlation_length rather than choosing it.”

Distances add. Survival multiplies.

Travel two units, then three more. Does that cost the same as travelling five in one go?

att(2) × att(3)  =  att(5)

Equal to the last digit the machine can hold — which is what the exponential is: adding in the exponent is multiplying outside it. A path's total attenuation is the product of its legs, and it does not matter where you put the joins.

One more thing falls out of this, and it is the single most quoted line in the design canon. Cooling over time uses the same kernel with the same shape:

cool(Δt, τ)  ≡  attenuate(d, ξ)

τ is tau and Δ is delta, meaning “a change in”. Checked directly: the two functions return bit-identical values — cool(7, tau=ξ) == attenuate(7, length=ξ) is True.

So forgetting is cooling is not a poetic gloss. Distance through space and distance through time are the same arithmetic, and the platform implements them once.

Mechanism two — an intersection along a path

Nothing to do with distance. When authority travels through a system it passes through a chain of permissions, and at each step you keep only what both sides agree on.

Nine actions — create, read, update, delete, evict, invoke, add, share, admin — are packed into the bits of a single whole number, so the whole operation is one instruction: a & b. That matters more than it sounds. “No” wins by construction, in the operator itself.

Here is where it bites. An edge records what it passes on as a short piece of text, and the decoder tests whether an action's name appears in it:

the edge sayswhich decodes to lets “read” through?

"r" looks like it says read. It says nothing — because “read” is not inside “r”. And "no-delete" reads as allowing delete, because “delete” is inside it.

The first is safe: it fails toward giving nothing away, and it has been left exactly as it is, because “fixing” it would silently widen every live edge that carries it. The second is the reason nothing in the system is allowed to write a phrase like that.

“No” is absorbing

A permission record can carry a deny: the same nine flags, but meaning “specifically not these”. Read such a record by asking it “can you read?” the naive way and you get a dangerous answer:

how it is readanswer
ask the record's can_read flag directlyTrue — a deny read as permission
build the mask, then askMask(deny:admin,read) → False
compose it along a path with two full-authority stepsMask(deny:admin,read)

That first row was a real defect. One half of the system knew a deny was absorbing; the path that walks the permission graph did not, and would hand out authority a deny had explicitly taken away. The fix was not to patch the check — it was to make the disagreement unrepresentable by giving both halves one operator.

The laws, checked over every one of the 1024 possible masks:

lawholds
a & a = a  (idempotent)True
a & b = b & aTrue
full authority changes nothingTrue
a deny anywhere empties the pathTrue
the result is never bigger than either sideTrue

The one difference that matters

Both mechanisms shrink. Both compose along a path. Neither can grow. One test separates them cleanly.

Do it twice. Does anything change?

authority

a & a  =  a

Intersecting a permission with itself changes nothing, ever. Checked across all 1024 masks: True. Asking the same question twice is asking it once.

propagation

att(d)²  =  att(2d)

Travelling the same distance twice attenuates twice. It never returns what you started with.

Authority is a set, with no distance and no in-between. Propagation is a rate over a distance. Reach for & on a propagation path, or for e−d/ξ on a chain of permissions, and nothing will raise — you will simply get a wrong answer that looks fine.

The library records this about itself. Both operations were once importable under the same bare name, and one was renamed specifically because “that is exactly the shape a wrong import takes.”

The other two, briefly

Three — a crossing attenuates nothing at all. Unit A6 measured this: the energy going into a crossing equals delivered plus reflected plus bystanding, at a residual of exactly zero. The projection is orthogonal, and orthogonal projections conserve. Loss does not belong at the meeting point; it belongs to the travel between meeting points, where it is charged against a measured distance.

Four — τ, the mismatch of apertures. Also from A6: a beam sent into a receiver with less room loses the excess, in exact proportion to the ratio of the two phase spaces. There is no distance anywhere in that, and no time. It is purely a matter of what will fit.

Four mechanisms, four different arithmetics, and no error message anywhere between them. Telling them apart is the entire content of this unit.

One ceiling over all four

what comes out  ≤  what went in

≤ is less than or equal to. Every one of the four obeys it, in its own currency:

  • Survival across a distance is at most 1, and is 1 only at zero distance.
  • An intersection is never larger than either side it came from.
  • A crossing delivers at most what it was handed, and manufactures no brightness.
  • τ is capped at 1 — you cannot gain room by being sent to.

Stated once, it is the sentence the whole construction is built on: composition along a path is monotone and non-amplifying. Authority can only be lost, never gained. Energy can only be spent, never conjured. A record can only be forgotten, never spontaneously remembered.

This is why it is worth being exact about which mechanism you are in. They are not four implementations of one rule — they are four models of one rule, and a model has its own arithmetic. Sharing a ceiling does not make them substitutable.

And the constant is measured, not assumed

All of the above needs a number from somewhere. It comes from the record. The attenuation constant is how far the strongest pattern in a record stands above the next one — a separation, read off the record's own spectrum.

On noise the constant reads exactly 0.000000. Not 0.003. Not “small”. Exact zero is the result.

But that column is one noise record, and a claim about what the instrument does is a claim about more than one. So the same read was run on 100 fresh noise records:

on 100 noise recordsresult
attenuation exactly zero100 of 100
modes above the floor = 0100 of 100
contrast, exactly zero0 of 100 — it reads 0.8624 to 0.9547

Two of the three read exact zero every single time. The third returns a number on every draw: contrast always returns a number, and on pure noise that number is not small — it sits in a narrow band from 0.8624 to 0.9547. The EEG reads 1.3194, which is above the whole band, but only 1.38× its top. So contrast separates the record from noise, by a margin. Attenuation and the mode count do something different in kind: they go to exact zero and stay there.

That is the practical reason this unit takes its constant from attenuation. A read that returns 0.91 on noise gives you a margin to argue about. A read that returns 0.000000 on a hundred noise records out of a hundred has answered the question.

Which is the moment to be exact about the other exact zeros this course has shown you, because they are three different kinds of claim wearing the same zero:

wherewhat the zero is
unit A5, redundancy on the marketa property of that record — no draw involved, so it is the same every time by construction
unit A6, coupling strength on a scrambled paira rate: exactly zero in 192 of 200 shuffles, and the slide there says so

A read that can only return small numbers invites you to believe in weak effects. A read that returns exactly zero has told you something. That is a design decision, repeated deliberately across the library — and the habit worth taking from the whole programme is the line above it: find out which kind of zero you have before you quote it.
