## The discipline this tree is written under, and why it is worth copying

`the-information-universe.md` opens with an honesty key, and it is the sharpest statement of method
anywhere in the corpus:

> *"The rule we have lived by: **if we can calculate it cleanly, with no fitting and no free knobs,
> it is real. The moment we need to tune a number to make it work, we are fooling ourselves.** That
> rule is also the thesis. The universe, we suspect, has no free knobs either."*

That is the guarantee — *measured, never set, never chosen* — arrived at independently, in the
research tree, and stated better than the brand documents state it. **Every claim in that document
carries its tag**, so a reader can separate what was measured from what is being bet on. Any surface
derived from this tree inherits that obligation.
