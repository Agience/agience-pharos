# Start here

**Agience is the substrate on which a finite observer's knowledge carries its own trust** — a lens
that measures any signal at its own resolution, a memory that proves where everything came from, and
a seat no one else controls. Every capability is a shareable, signed, content-addressed artifact.
Nothing is a trained weight. Meaning is computed by measurement, and every exchange of energy is
measured rather than set.

No observer sees the whole. Everything anyone knows arrives through a lens — the senses, an
instrument, an organisation's meetings, a model's training set. Trust is the technology finite
observers use to act on what they did not personally verify, and generative intelligence raised the
volume of claims while severing them from their evidence. The scarce thing now is the structure that
makes a claim trustworthy.

**Information carries through space, and through time.** Entroptics carries it through space; Mantle
carries it through time; Agience is the one who is looking. Three names, one coordinate frame, and
the observer is its origin. One guarantee holds them together: **measured, never set, never chosen.**
